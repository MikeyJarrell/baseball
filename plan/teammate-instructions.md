# Getting the Scorebook on your iPhone

Hey! Here’s how to get the scorebook app for while I’m away. It takes about ten minutes. You’ll get two emails from Apple at the address you use for your Apple account (the one your iPhone is signed in with). Do these steps on your iPhone.

**1. Accept the first email.**

It’s from App Store Connect, titled something like “You’ve been invited to join a team.” Tap “Accept Invitation”, sign in with your Apple account, and agree to the terms. That’s all this one is for. You won’t need that website again.

**2. Install TestFlight.**

Open the App Store, search for “TestFlight” (it’s made by Apple, with a blue propeller icon), and install it. It’s free. TestFlight is how Apple lets people install apps that aren’t in the App Store yet.

**3. Open the second email.**

It’s from TestFlight, titled something like “Scorebook is now available to test.” Tap “View in TestFlight”. TestFlight opens. Tap “Accept”, then “Install”.

If the second email hasn’t come yet, wait a few minutes, check your junk folder, and text me if it still isn’t there.

**4. Open the Scorebook.**

It’s on your home screen. Our team, the opponents, everyone’s walk-up songs and the announcer intros are already in it.

**Good to know**

- Turn the volume up for the walk-up songs. They keep playing if the phone locks.
- If TestFlight says there’s an update, tap “Update”. Your games stay.
- After each game, tap “Send games” and text me the file, so the games count toward our season stats.
- If the app ever says it has expired, text me and I’ll send a new copy.

Thanks for covering!

Mikey
