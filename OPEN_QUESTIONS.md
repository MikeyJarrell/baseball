# Open questions

Questions for Mikey. Delete each one once it is answered and the answer is written into the plan.

## Scorebook app

1. **How many innings is a game?** The pregame screen asks every game; this sets the starting number.

2. **Dropped third strike.** Can a batter run to first when the catcher drops strike three?

3. **Free substitution in the field.** Can players move in and out of fielding positions freely, the way long-lineup leagues often allow? If so, the app keeps fielding stats (putouts and assists) by position rather than by player.

4. **Courtesy runners.** When a courtesy runner scores, who gets credit for the run: the runner, or the player they ran for?

5. **Songs for the other team.** Always silent for their hitters, or sometimes something generic?

6. **Anything else unusual** in the league rules, beyond adding hitters to the bottom of the order, courtesy runners, re-entry and the mercy rule or time limit?
